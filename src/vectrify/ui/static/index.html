Snap edges

Automatically match touching edges of the selected paths, pair by pair. Of each touching pair the front contour stays in place; the one behind snaps to it. Regions keep their own colors.

Edges are split where needed, then the rear path's points and handles move onto the front path's. The paths stay independent afterwards. Pins and locks still apply.

Tidy

A quick clean-up of the selected paths: snaps their points onto the reference's edges and removes the points they do not need. Each round tries every chosen step and keeps the one that fixes most where it acted; Simplify runs when nothing else helps. Without a reference, Simplify removes points while keeping the paths looking the same. To reshape a path, use Redraw outline (R).

Moves the points onto the reference's nearest edges.

Removes the points the outline does not need, and the handles that lie on their line. Runs when no other step helps.

Fits points and curve handles to the reference by gradient descent, on the GPU when there is one. Straight segments can gain handles where the reference curves. Slow on large paths, so off by default.

Pinned points stay fixed. Colour is left to Fit colours.

Generate from reference

Traces the reference into new shapes, added as one group at the front.

Settings

Existing objects are not changed. Text in the reference is traced as shapes.

Fit colours

Solves the fill of each selected object that best matches the reference, including opacity, clipping and objects in front: a flat colour, or a linear gradient where the reference shades across the shape. Geometry stays fixed.

Settings

Several passes help when fitted objects overlap each other.

Clean up

Removes duplicate and collinear vertices, empty and duplicate paths, and merges compatible neighbouring paths into compound paths. Coordinates are never rounded.

What changes

Only selected objects change. Paths referenced by instances or clips are kept as they are.

Keyboard shortcuts

Every edit is one undoable step. Automated tools show a preview first; nothing changes until you press Apply. Ctrl/⌘ K finds any command by name.

V / C
Object tools: select, move and resize / knife
A / R
Point tools: Nodes / Redraw outline. They show the points of every selected path, and of every path inside a selected group
D
Draw path. Tool keys sit under the left hand and switch at once, even while an edit runs, cancelling a drag under way
Selecting
A click picks the outermost group; double-click a group to pick within it, or a path to edit its points. Drag on the canvas to box select what lies wholly inside: objects, or points in a point tool. Shift adds or removes.
Escape
Cancel the current drag, path or hole inspection; otherwise step up one level: points, then their path, its group, nothing
While an edit runs
Keys, clicks and drags wait for it and then apply in order
Switching tools
Keeps the objects selected. Points hide in object tools and come back in a point tool while the same objects are selected
Right-click
The selection's actions, on the canvas or in the object tree
Drawing a path
Click for corners; drag for curved handles. Click the first point to close, or Enter to finish open. Backspace removes a point; Escape cancels.
Knife
Drag a straight line across filled shapes, from outside to outside, to cut them in two along the line (with a selection, only the selected ones); the pieces meet exactly and Join merges them again. Across a stroked line it cuts the line where it crosses: cut across a loop to take it off as its own path. Shift snaps the line to 15° steps.
Fixing traced lines
In Nodes: Break cuts a line at the selected points, or with the two points at a segment's ends removes that segment, Join joins two selected points. In Actions: Join joins the selected lines' ends that continue each other within 12 screen pixels (zoom out to reach further) and merges filled paths, and Convert line/fill turns a thin filled shape into a stroke down its middle and a stroke into a filled shape.
Redraw outline
Start on a path's outline (or on one of its points; with a selection, a selected path's), draw roughly along the reference's edge and end on the same outline. The stretch between the two ends, the shorter way round and shown dashed, is replaced by curves fitted to the edge near the stroke; without a reference, to the stroke itself. Hold Shift to replace the longer way round instead.
1 – 9
Press the tool strip's numbered controls. In Nodes: 1 / 2 / 3 give the selected points no handle, one handle or both (2 again switches sides), 4 Break, 5 Join, 6 Split edge. In Select: 1 – 4 send to back, backward, forward, to front
Dragging points
The selected points move together, with their handles. They snap to other points and to the artboard's edges and corners; hold Alt or Ctrl / ⌘ to drag freely.
Resizing
In Select, drag an edge of the selection's frame to resize along that axis, or a corner for both; the cursor shows which. Shift keeps the aspect ratio, Alt resizes from the centre, and edges snap to other objects and the artboard (Ctrl / ⌘ drags freely). Strokes keep their width
